# Doom Fest

**Doom Fest** is a card game that pushes your luck and evilness to the limit! 

Can you help **Younickious the Witch** reach the top of the Doom List?

---

### How to Play
Hover your mouse over the card you want to play, left-click, and drag it into the arena.
